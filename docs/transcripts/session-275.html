Session 275 of 278: The framework calls in the Mechanic without the person

Scope: packages/router -- config.ts (the dabbler.mechanic and dabbler.mechanicModel settings through the layers), cli/configure.ts and dabbler configuration explain (the Mechanic Model), drive.ts (engaging on a stop, the budget, the undone attempt, the hand-back), engines.ts (launching a fresh engine CLI on the Mechanic Model), cli/consult.ts (the unattended mandate in the briefing); their tests

Why. The operator, 2026-10-21: the framework is the developer's chief of staff and the AI a consultant, and there should be situations where the chief of staff works directly with the consultant to troubleshoot without involving the CEO. Developers without Dabbler have this already, because nothing constrains their AI; the constraints stay, and the framework dictates what is allowable, so that the petty problems the framework and an AI can resolve between them never reach the developer. 231's non-goal "no unattended Mechanic" is superseded. Sol and Argon both recommended bounding it by the person's AUTHORITY rather than by a list of problem kinds: a list can only hold problems someone anticipated, and the walls that matter are the ones nobody did.

Step 1 -- the delegation is a setting. dabbler.mechanic: unattended (the default), attended or off, read through the same layers as every other choice and committed with the checkout. attended is 274's behaviour; off names no Mechanic at all.

Step 1b -- the Mechanic Model is its own choice. The developer's staff member, 2026-10-21: the Mechanic often needs a highly capable model even where the Authoring Model is a medium one, because it is called in exactly where the author got stuck. dabbler.mechanicModel is read through the same layers as dabbler.authoringModel -- a flag, this checkout's .vscode/settings.json, the machine's preferences.json -- and written by dabbler configure --mechanic-model <model>. The Mechanic runs on the authoring engine's CLI, so the model is one that engine accepts, and a choice the engine refuses is a stop naming the setting, as an Authoring Model's is. Unset, it is the Authoring Model, which meets the one criterion -- at least as capable as the author -- by being the author's. It may not be the session's Primary Model, for the rule that the Mechanic is never the Primary Reviewer. dabbler configuration explain names the layer that decided it.

Step 2 -- what it may not do. The briefing's unattended mandate is a deny-list of the person's authority: cancel, rollback, close, hold-release; any change to the plan or what counts as done; a significant design choice or an unapproved dependency; credentials, money, or anything outside the repository; a weakened test or gate; the record or a verdict. Anything else that gets the session moving again is allowed, as the minimum, with a stated reason why the fix keeps to the approved plan. A fix needing any of these is diagnosed and handed back without an edit. The enforcement is what exists: the router alone writes the record and moves the lifecycle, and every fix is reviewed by the Primary Reviewer and tested with the session's diff.

Step 3 -- the framework engages. In an ordinary session, unattended, a stop the framework would hand to a person first launches a fresh-context engine CLI on the Mechanic Model, never the author's live session. A funds stop (272) and a person's own interrupt are handed straight to the person. Routine rejections stay the author's.

Step 4 -- the budget, and a failed attempt undone. One attempt per stop, two per session, twenty minutes each. An attempt that runs out, or whose fix does not clear the stop, has the tree restored to what it was before the Mechanic started, with its diff kept in the log.

Step 5 -- the hand-back. What reaches the person is a memo on the stop: the cause and its evidence, what was tried, the line it reached, and one recommendation, with a plan amendment drafted where one is needed, for the person to approve.

Non-goals. Release sessions (277). The extension (276). Any guard machinery around the Mechanic -- no hashing, no sandbox -- beyond the record and the review that exist. A Mechanic that calls a Mechanic.

Tests. In config.test.ts, the two settings read through the layers, and a Mechanic Model equal to the Primary Model is refused.
