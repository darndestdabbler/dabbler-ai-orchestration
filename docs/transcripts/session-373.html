Step 1 -- the last fix gets its review. When the cap is reached and the author has changed the tree to answer the last round's blocking findings, the framework opens one more round over that change, once, and no report or flag asks for it. The cap still counts the rounds that may send work back: this round sends nothing back. If it finds nothing blocking the session is VERIFIED and carries on to its close.

Step 2 -- if that round still finds something. The session stops for the person, and the stop says what the reviewer still holds and gives the ways on, each with what it costs: the Auxiliary Reviewer judges the findings (dabbler verify adjudicate); the person buys further rounds (dabbler verify reopen); or the session closes as it is, and the stop says in these words that no release can include it until it has been reviewed again, with the command that does that.

Step 3 -- a change to the plan is shown as one. Where the change that answers a finding is to session-plan.md and to nothing else the finding cited, the round of step 1 is told so: what the section said when the finding was made, what it says now, and the recorded decision that changed it. The reviewer judges the work against the plan as amended and says whether the amendment answers the finding.

Step 4 -- the words. An ending at the cap that no reviewer read is never reported as passed: not in the run's events, the Dabbler Terminal, dabbler status or the transcript. It reads "ended at the cap, unreviewed", and a close that takes it says once, as it closes, that a release will refuse this session and names the command of session 372.

Step 5 -- the pages. docs/driving-a-session.md describes the cap as it now is, and loses what it says of the terminal a fix can no longer reach without a review. The changelog says it in a sentence.

Non-goals. Raising the cap, or a second review round past it. Sending a dispute to the Auxiliary Reviewer without a person: the refusal that routes a standing dispute there is unchanged. Refusing a plan amendment at the cap: a person may still narrow a plan, and the reviewer now reads it. Any change to the release start.
