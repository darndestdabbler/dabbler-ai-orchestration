Session 312 of 316: An exception is requested, checked and shown before a person authorises it

Scope: packages/router/src/cli/consult.ts (the Mechanic's brief, the consult brief), docs/sessions/mechanic-log.md (the entry's shape), packages/router/src/triage.ts and cli/triage.ts, packages/router/src/session.ts (the card), packages/router/src/driver.ts (a repeated stop), the Work Explorer's stop commands under tools/dabbler-ai-orchestration/src/, the tests named after each, docs/driving-a-session.md

Why. Session 311 gives a person the authority. This one makes sure what they are asked is fair, balanced and in plain language, so the decision is informed and takes a minute. The operator, 2026-11-11: in 95% of cases neither the author nor the Mechanic is trying to cheat -- there is a real blocker that needs an exception and possibly a framework change -- and what must not happen is that the person "is handed something that makes no sense." And they must be able to open an AI consult to help them decide, so everything said about an exception has to be somewhere a consult can read.

The operator proposed a ladder: reviewer, dispute, second look, the Auxiliary, then three positions. Round 23 cut it, and the operator agreed: ONE independent check, as advice and never as a gate on the person, with a second opinion when the person asks for one. Up to five model calls before a stuck developer may proceed is itself a wall, and what protects a person from being talked into an exception is a reviewer that reads the record for itself and a card that shows the rule's own words.

What.

Step 1 -- the request. The Mechanic's brief, attended and unattended, gains an instruction for the case its licence has never covered: where the wall is a rule and no fix within the rules clears it, do not work around it -- ask. The request is its mechanic-log.md entry, whose shape gains four lines: Rule in the way (in the rule's own words, from the stop), Why it is wrong here, What going past it lets through, and What was tried within the rules. An unattended Mechanic writes the request, says so in its memo and hands back. The entry's existing Deterministic fix line is the framework change the exception argues for.

Step 2 -- the check. dabbler triage already asks a provider that is not the author's to classify a stop from the record. Where a request stands for the stop it reads that too, and its answer gains three members: the within-rules path, as the exact commands or as "none found" -- never as "the request is right", because finding no alternative is not agreement; whether the request's account of the stop matches the record, and what differs where it does not; and whether a person who was not there could follow the request, and what is unclear. The answer is kept as an advice row in the run's exceptions.jsonl, with the adviser named and marked as advice. A named path is tried rather than argued: the Mechanic's reply to one is to run it and add what happened to its entry. A second opinion is the rung triage --provider already is, run when the person asks.

Step 3 -- the card. dabbler session waive with no --reason prints the card and changes nothing. The framework assembles it in a fixed order from parts it does not paraphrase, each attributed to who said it: the rule and what was unmet, from the record; the request, in the Mechanic's words; the advice, in the adviser's words -- leading with a within-rules path where one was named, and saying "no independent check was run" or "the adviser could not be reached" where that is so, neither of which refuses the waiver; and what the record will say once it is authorised.

Step 4 -- a consult can read it. With a stop standing, the consult brief says whether an exception has been requested, checked or authorised, and names the files: the log entry and the run's exceptions.jsonl. A consult opened to help the person decide reads what the person reads.

Step 5 -- the Work Explorer. A stopped session offers "Check this stop", which runs the triage and says before it does that it is one provider call, and "Review exception", which shows the card and takes the person's reason. A session closed past a waiver carries a small mark with the count, and its transcript opens at it. Where the same stop recurs on the same step, the stop's ways on lead with the check.

Step 6 -- the words. docs/driving-a-session.md says how an exception is requested, checked and authorised, and what each of the three roles says in it. The number and the changelog are session 313's.
