Session 308 of 311: A person may amend an accepted step's checks, and the engine is told so

Scope: packages/router/src/driver.ts (amendPlanStep, the rejected-thrice ways on), packages/router/src/session.ts (planAmend), packages/router/src/drive.ts (CHECK_FAILED_WAYS, the fix step's ask), packages/router/src/cli/session.ts (the help), the tests named after each, docs/driving-a-session.md, version.json and the manifests npm run stamp:version writes, tools/dabbler-ai-orchestration/CHANGELOG.md

Why. From the field, 2026-11-10: a session in another repository is stopped and has no way on but a cancel or a rollback. A check an earlier step declared no longer describes the tree -- the later work outgrew it, and the operator agreed it should change -- and dabbler session plan amend --step <id> --checks-file ... refused: *"step '<id>' has already been accepted; its report was measured against the step as it stood, and amending it now would move that bar afterwards"*.

The framework contradicts itself here. A fix step and a run-of-record fix are measured against allPlanChecks() -- every plan step's checks, each one an accepted step's by the time a fix step exists -- and three of its own sentences send the engine to the verb that refuses: the fix step's ask (*"A check an earlier step declared that your fix outgrows is amended first, not fought"*), CHECK_FAILED_WAYS, and the rejected-thrice ways on. So the check keeps failing, the amendment keeps being refused, the third refusal stops the session, and the stop names the same verb again. The refusal's reason is half right: the accepted report WAS measured against the old checks and nothing may rewrite that. But an accepted step's checks are not only history -- they are run again against every fix -- and a bar that is still being applied has to be movable by someone.

What. An accepted step's checks become amendable by a person, with the reason on the record and the row saying the step had been accepted. An engine is still refused, and is told what to do instead.

Step 1 -- the verb. amendPlanStep takes whether a person is asking (planAmend passes personIsPresent(), which already separates an engine's shell from a person's terminal or click). For an accepted step: --checks-file from a person is applied exactly as an unaccepted step's is -- through judgeCheckPrograms and writeWorkPlan -- and the amendments.jsonl row carries after_acceptance: true beside the reason, before and after, so the record shows the accepted report was measured against before. The activity-log line says "accepted step". --files for an accepted step stays refused for everyone, with its own sentence: the files were measured once and nothing measures them again. An engine asking for an accepted step's checks is refused in the words of isAPersonsVerb: report the step blocked, with the proposed checks and the reason in the notes, and the person runs the amendment -- the refusal prints that command whole, sessions directory and step filled in. The verb's success line says the checks every later fix is measured against are the new ones; the help in cli/session.ts stops saying "not-yet-accepted" without the exception.

Step 2 -- the sentences that named a verb that refused. The fix step's ask, CHECK_FAILED_WAYS and the rejected-thrice cost each say the true thing: a check of the step in hand is amended by the engine; a check an earlier, accepted step declared is the operator's to amend, and the engine reports blocked with the proposed checks. A failed check already carries its owner, so the rejection can say which of the two it is. The fix step in flight is measured against the amended checks on its next answer, not the ones it was issued with -- allPlanChecks() read again from the plan on disk, as amendedSpec already does for a plan step.

Step 3 -- the words and the number. docs/driving-a-session.md says, where it describes amending a step, who may amend an accepted step's checks and what the record keeps. version.json goes to 3.31.0, stamped with npm run stamp:version -- a minor, because the trunk this publishes already carries 305 to 307's maintenance posture -- and the changelog gets a 3.31.0 section saying what changed for a developer across 305 to 308.

Non-goals. Amending an accepted step's files, id or ask. Any change to an accepted report, a round or a verdict already written: the amendment moves what later fixes are measured against and nothing behind it. An engine or an unattended Mechanic amending an accepted step's checks. A Work Explorer command for the amendment. Showing amendments to the reviewer. Any new gate.
