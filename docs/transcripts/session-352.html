Session 352 of 362: The Dabbler Terminal's output in Visual Studio

Scope: tools/dabbler-visual-studio/ (the tool window and its view model)

Why. While a session runs, this is what a developer glances at, and at a stop it is what says what happened.

Step 1 -- the window. A dockable tool window, Dabbler Terminal, fed by dabbler terminal --follow --json: one row to a line, the time, the event and its fields, each piece coloured by the tone the router named, long lines wrapped under their first field as the terminal's are.

Step 2 -- a job's output is shown in a fixed-width face, as it came.

Step 3 -- following. The window stays at the newest line until a developer scrolls up, and returns to following when they scroll back to the end. Lines can be selected and copied.

Tests. In the library: a stream of line objects becomes rows in order, with a tone on each piece; a job's lines are marked as such.

Releasable. No.
