# The guide

Each page here takes one claim made about Dabbler and shows the mechanism
behind it, with the command or file where you can see it for yourself.
They are written for someone deciding whether to trust the product, and
each one can be read in one sitting. They are what the
[repository's front page](../../README.md) and the
[Marketplace listing](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=DarndestDabbler.dabbler-ai-orchestration)
link to for their proof. To run a session, read
[Driving a session](../driving-a-session.md); the files on disk are in the
[schema reference](../schema-reference.md).

- [The rules an AI works inside](discipline.md): one instruction at a time,
  declared files and checks, the framework measuring the work instead of
  the report, the gates at the close, and the verbs that are a person's.
- [Two models from different providers](verification.md): who reviews, what
  the reviewer is sent, findings, fixes and disputes, the cap, and why no
  verdict can be typed.
- [What is written down, and where](the-record.md): the files a session
  leaves and who writes each, the committed transcript, and how a person or
  a new model picks the work up.
- [Consult with AI, and the Mechanic](consult-and-mechanic.md): what
  happens when it gets stuck -- an AI to consult that drives nothing, a
  fresh AI that makes the smallest fix, what it may never do unattended, and
  the person's ways past a stop.
- [The Configuration](configuration.md): each row of the Solution
  Explorer's Configuration, where every choice is kept and which layer
  wins, the two vehicles, the model lists, and credentials.
